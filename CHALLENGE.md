# Rescue the Runaway AI Pipeline

Build Smarter. Waste Less.

Your company receives invoices, purchase orders, receipts and contracts. DocFlow
Rescue classifies them, extracts fields, validates them, and reports outcomes. Most
documents finish normally. Some work becomes unexpectedly expensive and may repeat
without delivering useful progress. Determine why using NetGreener and your coding
assistant, then verify a correctness-preserving improvement.

High duration alone does not prove waste. A large legitimate document can be costly.
Use resource observations, job identities, attempts, stages and outputs to support
your explanation. Automatic stuck-job alerts are not a promised feature of this challenge.

| Activity | Target minutes |
|---|---:|
| Clone and run | 5 |
| Discover and integrate NetGreener | 10 |
| Observe, collect baseline and investigate | 15 |
| Optimize | 18 |
| Verify and submit | 12 |
| Total | 60 |

Use these times as a guide. Prepare software downloads, account access and projects
and the private Azure `.env` before the event. Start baseline collection early;
reserve up to eight minutes in total for six controlled trials and upload checks. Perform the NetGreener integration during the competition.

Individuals and teams receive exactly the same task and rubric. Optional team roles
can separate telemetry, pipeline work, job orchestration and verification. Work on
separate modules to reduce merge conflicts, then use one final commit and evidence
set. Solo participants do not need additional branches or roles.

Required behavior includes all document types, fields, classifications, arithmetic,
source citations, valid recovery, legitimate terminal failures, changed content,
duplicate content, bounded attempts, cancellation and job lifecycle records. Details
are in `docs/DOCUMENT_FORMAT.md`. No hardcoded workload answers or skipped processing.

## Investigate with your agent

Use NetGreener observations to choose what to investigate. Ask your coding agent a
specific question, inspect its suggested change, and verify the result against both
application behavior and resource evidence. Distinguish NetGreener observations,
application records and your own inferences. A generated explanation is a hypothesis
until checked.

Keep three short decision records while working, using the template in
[SUBMISSION.md](SUBMISSION.md). Capture the observation, question, agent suggestion,
your decision and the evidence that checked it. Any suggestion may be accepted,
revised or rejected; judges reward the reasoning and verification, not disagreement
for its own sake. Record the AI tools/models used where known; full conversations
are not required.

## Demonstrate and defend

Freeze code and submission evidence at the end of the 60 minutes. Presentations follow
the coding period and do not extend it. Each presenting team has three minutes to
demonstrate its investigation and result, followed by two minutes of jury questions.
If attendance requires a shortlist, the organizers will announce the selection and
presentation arrangements before the event, using the same published rubric.

Show the original observation, your hypothesis, the targeted change, and the evidence
that required results remain correct. Judges will ask:

- What evidence made you investigate this part of the application?
- What could make your improvement look successful while actually being wrong?

They may also ask a question specific to your change. A team spokesperson should
answer in their own words without a live agent generating the defence. Judges assess
technical understanding, not accent or speaking confidence. Team members may help
explain decisions; individual contribution is not inferred from typing or commit counts.

The rubric is Innovation (25%), Efficiency (40%), Impact (20%) and Presentation (15%).
Independent correctness and measurement-coverage checks accompany judging. One overall
winning team is selected, including solo entrants as one-person teams. See
[RULES.md](RULES.md) for the jury, scoring, gates and tie-breaking.
